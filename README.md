This is another project that I created (vibe coded) through ZotGPT (UC Irvine's free AI tool for students, faculty, and staff). Even though I created this with ZotGPT, it is in no way, shape, or form representative of UC Irvine's views. This verison is still very much a demo, but it's in good enough shape that I thought I should add it to my website.

A recurring theme of my work and when I help people with their job searches is trying to identify at which point of the job search they are struggling. There are subjective reasons for why job seekers are getting rejected, but there are also a bevy of objective reasons and patterns that we can identify if the sample size is large enough. There is a lot of absolutist language being used in this tool, but it works well enough.

For this project, I needed to write a 2,300 word overview with most of the questions and analysis completed. I used ZotGPT to then create a design document with the full flowchart and different modifiers for routes/paths. The design document, and what was sent to ZotGPT to generate the initial HTML, was 4,000-ish words to generate 1,400 lines of code. Afterwards, I edited the HTML mostly for phrasing and changing many of the ENDs.

Thanks for reading and let me know if there are any issues,
